Green-circuit supplier · 360/min

Makes 360 electronic circuits per minute from iron and copper plates, on one red belt leaving the east side. One copy covers the essentials mall or robot essentials; six-pack science needs three.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

19 × 9 = 171 tiles · 61 entities · 10 assembling machine 2 · 1 substations · red transport · no modules

This is the circuit supplier for the other three essentials builds. One red belt carries four copies' output side-loaded from alternating sides, two per lane (each exit carries 270 per minute on its north lane and 90 on its south lane). See the pairing table.

Suppliers: Green circuits · Advanced circuits · Processing units · Plastic bars · Oil products

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material and connect electricity at P.
  4. Collect the finished goods on the east. A full chest or exit pauses that product on purpose.

Inputs

Nominal demand is the manifest figure on each entrance panel at the targets. Measured is what the native test drew during its 30-minute window at the measured rates. Plan supply for the Measured column, not the panel figure: products run above their targets and take material first, so a build fed at only the panel figures can starve its last products.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; 10 assembling machine 2 (6 copper cable, 4 electronic circuit), no modules, red belts, fast and bulk inserters, one substation. WEST: copper plates 540/min (upper entrance) and iron plates 360/min (lower entrance); both may arrive on both lanes, the blueprint side-loads each onto its own lane of the plate belt (row 4). EAST: one red belt exit on row 5, 360 electronic circuits/min (270 on the north lane, 90 on the south lane). Cable is handed machine to machine; keep the bulk arms (hand 2) at the outer cable machines and the fast arms at the middle one: the hand sizes fix the 2:1 cable split. Keep the plate filters on the input arms, the underground belt pairs and the dead-end curve at the east end of the plate belt. Supply electricity at the substation.
  • Belt the exit east to the circuit entrances of the consumers. The exit holds 8 circuits when the consumer stops taking them, and every machine then stops. Sharing one red belt (1,800/min, 900 per lane): side-load the copies onto it from alternating sides, two copies per lane, four copies per belt. Joined lane for lane, three copies fill the north lane (810 of 900).
  • Feed plates at or above the listed rates; extra plates simply back up on the entrance belts.

Reproduce

From factorio-command-center/ in the source repository:

export FACTORIO_RAW=<Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age data-raw-dump.json>
node scripts/green-circuits/generate.mjs        # writes blueprint-sources/green-circuits/green-circuits.txt and manifest.json
node --test tests/green-circuits.test.mjs
node scripts/green-circuits/validate.mjs        # full native protocol; needs a local Factorio 2.1.24 install, run headless with a private profile under .cache/green-circuits/
