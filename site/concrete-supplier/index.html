Concrete supplier · 45/min

Makes 45 concrete per minute from stone bricks, iron ore and water in one assembling machine 2, on a red belt leaving the bottom row of the east side. One copy covers the launch kit.

Native production test passed · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

5 × 6 = 30 tiles · 17 entities · 1 assembling machine 2 · 1 substation · red transport · no modules

← Space launch path · Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material at or above its panel figure, and connect electricity at P.
  4. Run the east exit belt straight to its consumer. When the consumer stops taking, the exit holds 8 and the machines stop by themselves.

Protocol: 15 min warm-up, 30 min measured, 5 min blocked (collection stopped, entrances still fed), 5 min settle, 10 min recovery. Every entrance was fed at its panel figure, not saturated. Blocked: concrete exit held exactly 8, and no machine crafted in the last minute.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; one assembling machine 2 making concrete, no modules, red belts, fast inserters and one long-handed inserter, one substation. WEST: stone bricks 22.5/min (top belt), water 450/min (pipe), iron ore 4.5/min (bottom belt); each belt may carry its item on either lane. EAST: one red belt exit on the bottom row, 45 concrete/min, on both lanes; it holds 8 when the consumer stops taking concrete, and the machine then stops by itself. Keep the two output arms where they are: one fills each lane of the exit. Supply electricity at the substation.
  • Place this supplier west of the launch kit with its exit row (row 5 from its top) on the kit's concrete entrance row and join them with a straight red belt. One copy covers the kit (the kit's concrete panel is 23 a minute).
  • Feed stone bricks, iron ore and water at or above the panel figures; extra simply backs up on the entrance belts and in the pipe.
