Factory alone · external pack collection

  1. Download or copy the factory import. In Factorio, choose Import string and place the complete blueprint.
  2. Build every machine, power pole, inserter, belt and pipe. Preserve filters, red wires, whole-belt readers and stock limits.
  3. Connect the west supplies below, in both banks: upper original core and lower purple bank. Feed all shown entrances, including all three copper ports. Connect external power at P.
  4. For the factory-alone import, collect the five packs from the east providers or route them to your own labs. Keep output storage clear while measuring production.

Research-speed upgrades reduce the number of labs that the same science supply can continuously feed. Your flight map may already run at effective 2× lab speed: use the right-hand column. This is lab research speed, not the game simulation speed setting. More labs can be placed, but they will share the available packs and run intermittently.

Effective speed includes the lab’s base research speed and your force’s laboratory speed modifier. With these normal, module-free labs, 2× means twice the base research rate. If the technology uses different pack quantities, divide each supply by its per-unit quantity before applying the bottleneck rate.

Research gates & first startup

This factory supplies red, green, blue, yellow and purple packs. Technologies requiring military, space or planetary packs also need those packs supplied separately.

  • Start with red and green science. Blue science unlocks the advanced recipes needed for yellow inputs: processing units, electric engines, flying robot frames and low-density structures. Unlock the ingredient recipes before enabling the complete yellow chain.
  • Purple science also needs the productivity-module, electric-furnace and rail recipes. Productivity modules are manufactured ingredients for purple science; no modules are installed in machines or labs. Substations are permitted; each card reports actual saved power-pole counts.
  • Research Automation 2, Logistics 2, Fast inserters, Advanced electronics, Circuit network, oil processing and the sulfuric-acid/lubricant chains, plus their prerequisites. Passive-provider chests need Logistic robotics.
  • Bulk inserter baseline: the bulk-inserter unlock itself grants +1 capacity in the source game version, giving a two-item hand. Build the marked bulk arms and research that unlock; merely enabling the recipe on an otherwise unresearched force is insufficient. No later inserter-capacity upgrade is assumed anywhere in this factory.
  • Let all five colours reach the farthest labs and let the internal inventories fill before judging the rate. The connected variants use a longer priming period in their native tests. If a colour stops, check power, all three copper feeds in both supply banks, the two fluids, all recipes unlocked, stock-control wiring and blocked east storage.
