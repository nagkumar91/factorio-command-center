Space launch kit · silo, pad, starter pack and platform parts

Builds one complete space launch kit and stops: the rocket silo, the cargo landing pad, the space platform starter pack, every part the white-science platform asks rockets for and the receiver's parts, each in its own capped chest on the east side. Take items out and exactly those are made again.

Native kit test passed · Passing full-protocol evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

32 × 27 = 864 tiles · 350 entities · 28 assembling machine 2 · 1 chemical plant · 6 substations · red transport · no modules

← Space launch path · Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material at or above its panel figure, and connect electricity at P.
  4. Leave the kit in its sixteen east chests (passive provider chests) and cover them with the same roboport network as the rocket silo; the kit makes no roboports or logistic robots (see the robot essentials or the robot logistics workshop). Take out by hand only what you build on the ground: the rocket silo and the white-pack receiver's parts. The silo's robots take the starter pack and the platform parts from the chests. Each chest stops at its cap; take items out and exactly those are made again.
  5. First launch: in a save that has never created a space platform, the asteroid collector and crusher recipes are locked until the first platform is created, so the kit fills 14 of its 16 chests and waits. Do not wait for a complete kit: place the silo and create the platform as soon as the silo and the starter pack are ready. The kit then makes the crusher and the four collectors by itself, and the last cargo rocket waits for them. The measured kit time below applies to a save where a platform already exists.

Inputs

The kit has no rate. Its native run fed every entrance at its panel figure and measured how much of each material one whole kit took. Per kit is the recipe demand of one kit; accepted is what the entrance took from empty to a complete kit; consumed leaves out what still sat on belts, the ring and in pipes when the kit was done. Stone brick, sulfuric acid and lubricant are judged on consumed, because their entrance belt or pipes alone hold more than 15% of one kit.

Protocol: Fill from empty within 90 minutes under throttled entrances; hold 15 minutes (no craft in the last 10); remove part of the kit and refill within 30 minutes; long hold 30 minutes (no craft in the last 20). The rocket silo, landing pad and starter pack were each crafted exactly once by the blueprint's own machines. Per-kit rule: measuredPerKit (accepted from start to kit completion) <= perKitTolerance x perKit. Where (perKitTolerance - 1) x perKit is smaller than the entrance's own static storage (staticStorage on the port: for a belt its dead-end entrance belt, 8 items per tile, plus its ring limit; for a fluid every pipe of that fluid plus one input fluid box of each machine using it) the rule is applied to consumedPerKit (accepted minus what still sits on belts, the ring, machine inputs, arm hands, pipes and fluid boxes at kit completion). With the current layout this applies to stone-brick, lubricant and sulfuric-acid.

Setup notes

  • Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age. Builds one launch kit and stops: rocket silo, cargo landing pad, space platform starter pack and the white-platform and white-pack-receiver items, each in its own passive provider chest on the east side, capped by one constant combinator (one negative number per item). 29 machines (assembling machine 2, one chemical plant for batteries), no modules. WEST inputs, one labelled entrance each: concrete (from the concrete supplier), plastic, iron, stone brick, sulfuric acid, lubricant, copper, steel, electronic circuits (from the green-circuit supplier). Take items out of a chest and exactly those are made again. Preserve the red ring wires, the green cap wires, the five latch deciders and their red wires, the two silo reserve deciders, the foundation-first decider, the foundation gate decider, the AM1 combinator and iron arm wires, and all arm filters and conditions. Supply electricity at the marked substation.
  • Feed each west entrance with its labelled material only. Concrete comes from one concrete-supplier and electronic circuits from one green-circuits copy (placed directly west, exit on the entrance row).
  • Caps live in one constant combinator at the east edge (one negative number per item). Each chest arm runs only while its item is below its cap; the five expensive finals (silo, pad, starter pack, collector, crusher) load exactly one craft at a time behind a latch decider, so they never take a second craft's ingredients.
  • Take items out of a chest and exactly those are made again; nothing else crafts.
