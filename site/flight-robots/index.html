Compact robot workshop

Factorio 2.1.24 · your flight-save recipes.

Target: 10 construction robots + 10 logistic robots + 1 roboport per minute, simultaneously. Finished-material inputs · AM2 · fast inserters · red belts · no modules or starter robots. Three chemical plants make batteries. Circuit network, robotics, logistic robotics and the ingredient recipes must be researched.

Compact robots · 10 + 10 robots and 1 roboport/min

96 × 18 tiles · 1728 tiles² · 45 AM2s + 3 chemical plants

Circuit network research required. Keep the red stock-control wires intact. One entrance per ingredient. All three products go to ONE shared steel chest. Collection caps: 200 construction robots, 200 logistic robots, 20 roboports. Leave the shared chest unrestricted; per-item inserter limits reserve space for every product.

Native verified: construction-robot 10.10/min · logistic-robot 10.13/min · roboport 1.00/min · Tested build ID robots-10-roboport-1

Tested design comparison

All listed layouts passed sustained simultaneous production and blocked-output recovery. The current version is the smallest and uses one machine fewer; the earlier 106 × 18 version measured a few more crafts; the higher-throughput version provides more spare capacity. These are the best verified choices among the candidates tested, not a proof of global optimality.
