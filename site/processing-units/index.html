Processing-unit supplier · 27/min

Makes 27 processing units per minute (measured 26.83) from copper plates, iron plates, plastic bars and sulfuric acid, and sends them out on the east side on one red belt. It makes its own intermediate parts inside.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

31 × 28 = 868 tiles · 333 entities · 35 assembling machine 2 · 6 substations · red transport · no modules

Suppliers: Green circuits · Advanced circuits · Processing units · Plastic bars

Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep the inserter filters, the underground pairs and the dead-end belts as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material (see Inputs) and connect electricity at the substation marked P, at tiles (5–6, 16–17).
  4. Take the processing units from the exit on the east, (30, 16). A full exit pauses the supplier on purpose.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; 35 assembling machine 2 (12 copper cable, 9 electronic circuit, 8 advanced circuit, 6 processing unit), no modules, red belts, fast, long-handed and bulk inserters, six substations. WEST: copper plates 1080/min, iron plates 648/min, sulfuric acid 135/min (pipe), plastic bars 108/min; each belt may carry its item on both lanes. EAST: one red belt exit, 27 processing units/min. Cable, circuits and advanced circuits are handed machine to machine; extra circuits and plastic reach the advanced-circuit and processing-unit machines on the supply belt in the middle channel. Keep the plate filters, the underground belt and pipe-to-ground pairs and the processing-unit machines facing west (acid enters from the west gap). Supply electricity at any substation.
  • Belt the exit east to the processing-unit entrance of the consumer. The exit holds 8 processing units when nothing takes them, and every machine then stops.
  • Feed plates, plastic and acid at or above the listed rates; extra simply backs up at the entrances.

Power

The blueprint holds 6 substations, linked to one another by 6 copper wires. Connect electricity at the substation marked P, at tiles (5–6, 16–17): run a wire from your network to it, or place the module inside a substation’s reach of an existing pole. This report does not record the electric power the module draws.
