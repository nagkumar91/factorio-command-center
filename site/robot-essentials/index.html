Construction robots, logistic robots, roboports, storage chests, passive provider chests and requester chests at 5 / 5 / 0.5 / 2 / 2 / 2 per minute, in a smaller footprint than the robot logistics workshop. Batteries, engines, frames and advanced circuits are made inside.

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material and connect electricity at P.
  4. Collect the finished goods on the east. A full chest or exit pauses that product on purpose.

Setup notes

  • Factorio 2.1.25 Space Age, normal quality: AM2 and chemical plants, no modules, red belts, fast/bulk inserters. WEST entrances: electronic circuits, iron, copper, steel, plastic (belts) and sulfuric acid, lubricant (pipes). EAST: one passive provider chest with independent caps 50 construction robots, 50 logistic robots, 10 roboports, 50 storage chests, 50 passive provider chests, 50 requester chests (the requester chests come straight from their own machine beside it through a gated inserter). Preserve the red ring wires (per-tile hold readers), the green and red chest wires, the token wires, filters and caps. Supply electricity at either substation; the two are wired together.
  • Supplied electronic circuits: 155 per minute at the nominal target, one green-circuits copy (360 per minute) covers it.
  • The entrance panels are the supply to plan for. Fed at exactly the panel figures the workshop makes about 4.9 construction robots, 4.9 logistic robots, 0.5 roboports and 2.0 of each of the three chests per minute (token pace 4.9 / 4.88 / 0.51 / 1.97 / 1.97 / 1.97), all above the 95% floors; panel-supply-report.json is that full-protocol run. With more supply than the panels a boost of one eighth switches on while the ring is well stocked; with saturated entrances the full protocol measured 5.53 / 5.47 / 0.53 / 2.2 / 2.2 / 2.2 per minute and drew about 176 electronic circuits, 120 iron, 110 copper, 101 steel, 88 plastic, 450 sulfuric acid and 169 lubricant per minute. Below the panel figures the roboport falls short first. The supply level at which the boost starts (about 1.25 times the panel figures) was measured for the five-product string and has not been measured again.
  • Leave the shared chest unrestricted (no bar). Caps (50 construction robots, 50 logistic robots, 10 roboports, 50 storage chests, 50 passive provider chests, 50 requester chests): the first five live in the five filter deciders next to the chest, the requester chests in the permission decider of the inserter that fills the chest from its machine; the chest has 48 slots and the six products at their caps fill six.
  • The ring holds at most the stock limits in loopControl; do not remove the red wires from ring tiles or the entrance belts, which are circuit-gated. The requester chest machine hands its product to the chest through the inserter at the chest’s west side; keep that inserter and its red and green wires.

Reproduce

From factorio-command-center/ in the source repository:

export FACTORIO_RAW=<data-raw-dump.json of the Factorio Space Age version the native report names>
node scripts/robot-essentials/generate.mjs        # writes blueprint-sources/robot-essentials/robot-essentials.txt and manifest.json
node --test tests/robot-essentials.test.mjs
node scripts/robot-essentials/validate.mjs        # full native protocol; needs a local install of the Factorio version the native report names, run headless with a private profile under .cache/robot-essentials/
