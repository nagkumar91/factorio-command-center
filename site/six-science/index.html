Six-pack science · 30/min each

Automation, logistic, military, chemical, production and utility science packs at 30 per minute each, from supplied plates, bricks, stone, coal, sulfur, plastic, electronic circuits and two fluids. Space science stays with the orbital cell.

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material and connect electricity at P.
  4. Collect the finished goods on the east. A full chest or exit pauses that product on purpose.

Setup notes

  • Makes automation, logistic, military, chemical, production and utility science packs at 30/min each from supplied iron, copper, steel, stone brick, stone, coal, plastic, sulfur, electronic circuits, sulfuric acid and lubricant (all entrances on the west, one per material, each labelled with what it draws). Six passive provider chests on the east hold 200 of each pack. A governor beside the chests (four combinators) hands over one pack of each kind every 2 seconds, starting 10 minutes after it is powered, so the build fills first and then draws what the panels state. Advanced circuits, engines, frames, processors, low-density structures, rails, furnaces and modules are made inside. Keep every belt reader, red and green wire, combinator and inserter condition.
  • Supply both lanes of every west entrance belt; the copper entrance carries 1,035/min.
  • Needs 900 electronic circuits per minute: 3 copies of the green-circuits supplier (360/min each).
  • Power enters at the marked substation; one electric network.
  • The panels are the supply to plan for. The governor hands over 30 of each pack per minute (one every 120 ticks per chest arm) and every stock upstream is capped, so a full build draws the recipe totals on the panels and no more. The qualification is two full-length runs of this exact string: every entrance offered a full belt, and every entrance limited to exactly its panel figure; the status is qualified only when both passed (native-report.json, builds and panelSupply).
  • Start: the governor hands over nothing for the first 10 minutes after its combinators are powered, so the rings and machines fill at full speed before packs leave. With supply already connected the build then holds 30/min of every pack at the panel figures. If supply arrives after those minutes the build still settles at 30/min each, but while it fills the last packs on the shared spines run a little short (exploration run of a copy without the start delay, supply exactly the panel figures: production 28.6/min, utility 29.2/min and military 29.6/min over minutes 15 to 45, every pack 29.9 to 30.4/min over minutes 60 to 70).
  • After a pause (full chests, no collection) at most 10 periods are saved per pack, so at most 10 extra packs of each kind follow before the rate is 30/min again. Fed below the panel figures the last packs on the shared spines fall first (exploration, 0.95 of every panel: production 26.3/min, utility 28.7/min, the other four 30.0/min).

Reproduce

From factorio-command-center/ in the source repository:

export FACTORIO_RAW=<Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age data-raw-dump.json>
node scripts/six-science/generate.mjs        # writes blueprint-sources/six-science/six-science.txt and manifest.json
node --test tests/six-science.test.mjs
node scripts/six-science/validate.mjs        # full native protocol; needs a local Factorio 2.1.24 install, run headless with a private profile under .cache/six-science/
