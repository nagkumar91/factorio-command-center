Plastic bar supplier · three sizes

Makes plastic bars from coal on one belt and petroleum gas in one pipe, and sends them out on the east side on one red belt, in three sizes: small 240 per minute, medium 600 per minute, large 1,200 per minute. The medium size is the one to start from; the pairing table says which size each library blueprint needs.

Native production tested · all three sizes passed their full native protocol for their exact import string.

Suppliers: Green circuits · Advanced circuits · Processing units · Plastic bars · Oil products

Open in the library

Petroleum gas: the oil products supplier makes it (7,140 per minute from one copy), gas port to gas port: its outlet at (42, 7) to this supplier's gas inlet at (0, 0). One copy feeds a small or a medium size; 2 copies feed a large.

One substation per module, at tiles (6–7, 2–3), marked P on the layout. Connect your electric network to it; the one substation covers the whole module. These reports do not record the electric power a size draws.

Reproduce

From factorio-command-center/ in the source repository:

node scripts/plastic-bars/generate.mjs                      # writes the three strings and manifest.json
node --test tests/plastic-bars.test.mjs
node scripts/plastic-bars/validate.mjs --size small         # full native protocol, writes native-report-small.json (also medium, large); needs a local Factorio 2.1.24 install, run headless with a private profile under .cache/plastic-bars/
