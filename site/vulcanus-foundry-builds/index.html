Good to know

  • One blueprint for Vulcanus. Foundries turn lava and calcite into molten iron and molten copper and cast plates, steel, gears, pipes, sticks, cable, low density structures and concrete; assembling machines 3 make the rest. Everything is kept at a cap in five passive provider chests on the south edge, and made in the order of the list: a group lower on the list waits while a group above it has something it can make.
  • Rocket parts (the 50 processing units, 50 low density structures and 50 rocket fuel per launch) are not part of this blueprint.
  • Power: connect the substation at tile (3, 8) (panel "POWER: connect this substation"). Four substations power everything. Idle draw is small; with all foundries working it reaches about 25 MW.
  • Stone is a by-product of every lava craft. The bricks the bill needs are made inside (a five-stack brick stock); the rest leaves on the belt of the stone exit and must be taken away (run that belt to an inserter over lava). If the exit backs up, the two lava foundries stop and so does everything else; it carries on once the belt moves again (tested for an hour of blockage). About 93% of the stone is surplus: there is nothing in this bill to spend it on.
  • Water and lubricant each pass a storage tank the control reads; a fluid counts as present above 500. The tanks take 25,000 each when first connected.
  • Other inputs needed besides lava and calcite: plastic bar, tungsten carbide (foundries only), sulfuric acid (processing units, batteries), lubricant (electric engines, express belts, foundries), water (concrete, refined concrete). No oil or tungsten chain is inside.
  • The asteroid collector and crusher recipes exist only once a space platform has been created. On Vulcanus that is always the case.
  • Tiny recipes can run one craft over their cap (an arm's second item is a full set). The extra rides the ring, counts as stock and goes into the chest after the next collection.
  • Kit items enter the kit chest only while no assembling machine 3 is wanted.
  • Collecting from a chest reopens that product's level; lower groups pause until it is full again.
  • Connect the stone exit. A stone belt that cannot move stops the two lava foundries, and with them everything; nothing is harmed and the mall carries on when the belt moves again.
  • The brick stock (5 stacks) is filled from surplus stone whenever metal is cast, so concrete can be made later when little stone is flowing.

Good to know

  • Calcite has two entrances: a belt on the north edge for the mall part (it rides the ring to the two lava foundries) and a belt on the west edge for the fuel section (one lane of the coal belt). They are not connected inside.
  • Water has one entrance for both parts, on the east edge: the fuel section's crackers and the casting foundry (concrete) and the refined concrete machine draw on it.
  • Sulfuric acid and coal feed only the rocket fuel section. The mall part needs neither.
  • Power: connect the substation at tile (33, 18) (panel "POWER: connect the substation above"). Five substations in one chain power everything.
  • Stone is a by-product of every lava craft. The bricks for concrete are made inside; the rest leaves on the belt of the stone exit and must be taken away. If that belt cannot move, the two lava foundries stop and so does the mall part; nothing is harmed, and it carries on when the belt moves again (tested for an hour of blockage). The rocket fuel section is not affected.
  • Lubricant passes a storage tank the control reads; it counts as present above 500. The tank takes 25,000 when first connected.
  • One foundry casts all five iron products. It keeps up with this list; it would not keep up with the big mall's kit.
  • The buffer chest can hold intermediates for a long time when the ring is at its limits. They count as stock and are used first when the ring has room.
  • There is no water tank: nothing in the control waits for water. With water missing the casting foundry may sit on the concrete recipe while nothing else is asked of it; plate, gear and stick still come first, and only foundries (and rocket fuel) need water.
  • Collecting from a chest reopens that product's level; lower groups pause until it is full again. Rocket fuel is never paused by this.
  • The rocket fuel section's figures hold with all three refineries fed: coal 352, calcite 71, sulfuric acid 880, water 1320 a minute.
