SMELTING · EXACT SAVED LAYOUT

Electric smelter · ore through east, plates south

24 electric furnaces smelt iron or copper ore into 900 plates per minute with no modules (versions with efficiency, productivity and speed modules below), one full yellow belt leaving the south side. Ore comes in on the west; the station takes what it needs first and the rest carries straight on to the east side on the same row, so copies placed side by side chain along one ore line: one red belt feeds two.

Native production tested · 6 of 6 scenarios passed for this exact import (Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age).

30 × 20 = 600 tiles · 212 entities · 24 electric furnaces, no modules · 38 inserters, 10 fast inserters · 4 substations · 2 roboports

Derived from In-Out Electric Smelter in the Autosaved collection (id autosaved-f3d991e31c13c301, https://autosaved.org/factorio/blueprints); the original string is kept here. Open the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Bring ore on a red belt to entrance 1 on the west (row 1, the second row from the top).
  3. Take plates from exit 3 on the south; leave exit 2 on the east for the next copy, or unconnected.
  4. Connect electricity to any substation. The main string requests no modules; pick a version below for modules.

Chaining stations

  • Place the next copy 30 tiles east, touching. Its entrance takes the forwarded ore with no extra belts.
  • Neighbouring copies join one electric network through their own substations, and their roboports do not collide.
  • Red belt: two copies at full rate (measured 900 and 900 per minute). Yellow belt: one copy.
  • Blue belt: enough ore for three copies, but the ore line inside each copy is red and passes at most 1,800 per minute, so as built it still feeds two. Upgrade each copy's entrance splitter, row-1 belts and its underground pair to blue to feed three (belt-speed arithmetic, not run).

Measured

One headless protocol, every scenario on its own surface: 10 min warm-up, 10 min measured, 10 min blocked phase (scenario 4 only), 3 min settle, 10 min recovery. Pass bar 882 plates per minute and every furnace within 2% of its share. The modules the string requests (two per furnace, or none) inserted into every furnace by script; item-request proxies removed; module inventories read back at the end of the measured window. No research: inserter hand size 1 (force inserter_stack_size_bonus 0). One electric energy interface north of copy 1 only. Power drawn is the electric network input statistics over the measured window of a single copy (furnaces, inserters and roboports).

What changed from the original

  • Original ports: Ore in and plates out both on the west side; no ore leaves the station, so copies cannot share one ore line without extra belts.
  • Ore now runs through: a red belt along row 1 from the west edge to the east edge, starting with a red splitter whose priority output feeds the station.
  • Plates leave on the south edge (last column) instead of the west edge: the two plate lanes now flow east and merge in a splitter on the east side.
  • The first splitter of the ore split became a red splitter fed from above by a red belt; the other two splitters and all four ore lanes are unchanged.
  • Same 24 furnaces, same 38 inserters and 10 fast inserters, same 4 substations and 2 roboports on the same tiles. 30 x 20 instead of 28 x 20: two columns more on the east side for the plate exit; the west side keeps its width (the entrance splitter stands where the old input belt was, the west plate merge is gone).
  • Module requests: the original asked for 2 efficiency modules in each furnace. The main string now has none; efficiency, productivity and speed versions are kept beside it.
