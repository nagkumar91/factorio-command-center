Production science, small · 12.9/min

Makes 12.86 production science packs per minute (measured 12.8) from steel plates, stone bricks, stone, iron plates, electronic circuits and advanced circuits, and sends them out on the east side on one red belt. It makes its own intermediate parts inside.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

14 × 18 = 252 tiles · 117 entities · 7 assembling machine 2 · 1 substation · red transport · no modules

Circuits from: electronic circuits (21.4 per minute) from the Green-circuit supplier, which makes 360 per minute; advanced circuits (42.9 per minute) from the Advanced-circuit supplier, which makes 187.5 per minute.

Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep the inserter filters, the underground pairs and the dead-end belts as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material (see Inputs) and connect electricity at the substation marked P, at tiles (2–3, 8–9).
  4. Take the production science packs from the exit on the east, (13, 6). A full exit pauses the module on purpose.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; 7 assembling machine 2 (2 production science pack, 2 productivity module, 1 electric furnace, 1 rail, 1 iron stick), no modules, red belts, fast and long-handed inserters, one substation. WEST: steel plates 107.1/min, stone bricks 42.9/min, stone 64.3/min, iron plates 32.1/min, electronic circuits 21.4/min, advanced circuits 42.9/min, each on its own entrance belt (both lanes allowed); inside, every two share one belt, one per lane. EAST: one red belt exit, 12.9 production science packs/min. Furnaces, modules and rails are handed straight into the science machines, sticks straight into the rail maker. Keep the underground pair under the exit. Supply electricity at the substation.
  • Belt the exit east to the labs. The exit holds 4 packs when nothing takes them, and every machine then stops.
  • Feed every entrance at or above its panel figure; extra simply backs up at the entrances. The circuits can come from the green-circuit and advanced-circuit suppliers.
