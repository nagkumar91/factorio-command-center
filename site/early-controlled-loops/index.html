Fast science, raw feeders and controlled loops

Faster research: open the NEW compact solid-fuel science book (complete connected factory + separate compact feeder and science loop).

Open all blueprints as one book. Individual import strings and exact layouts are below. The upgraded science loop targets 30 red and 30 green packs per minute using AM2s, red belts and fast inserters. Its dedicated raw feeder supplies the necessary plates and green circuits. The earlier AM1 workshops and original feeder remain below for smaller builds. No modules are needed; optional ingredient taps can stay empty.

Circuit network research is required. A belt reader counts items circulating on the whole loop. Every external feed and assembler output has a red-wire condition: add its item only below the saved stock cap. Output collectors filter finished items into separate one-stack iron chests. The science chests are beside each other.

Connect electricity at the big pole. Feed the required west entrances. Optional east taps add gears, cables or sticks to the same stock pool; their internal producers pause automatically as stock rises. Leave optional taps empty to make those intermediates inside. Belt readings exclude ingredients already inside machines or held by inserters. Iron-hungry upstream recipes also wait for a minimum iron reserve, protecting downstream recipes. Belt arrows show travel; inserter arrows show delivery.

The chest limits make these on-demand workshops. Collect products to resume. After researching passive provider chests, upgrade the output chests for robot collection. Preserve the red wires and whole-belt reader.
