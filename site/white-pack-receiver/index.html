White-pack receiver · landing pad to chest

One cargo landing pad that requests space science packs, one fast inserter and one passive provider chest: the white-science platform drops pods by itself and the packs collect in the chest.

Native receiver test passed · Passing full-protocol evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

10 × 8 = 80 tiles · 4 entities: 1 × cargo landing pad, 1 × fast inserter, 1 × passive provider chest, 1 × substation

← Space launch path · Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string and build it on Nauvis, under the white-pack platform.
  2. A planet allows one cargo landing pad. If you already have one, add a request for 1,000 space science packs to it and build only the inserter, chest and substation next to it.
  3. Connect electricity at P. The platform drops a cargo pod every 30 seconds with what the pad lacks; no click is needed.
  4. Take packs from the chest (robots or by hand). It stops at its cap and everything resumes when packs are taken.

Setup notes

  • Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age. One cargo landing pad that requests 1000 space science packs, one fast inserter and one passive provider chest limited to 10 stacks (2000 packs). Build it on Nauvis under the white-pack platform: the platform drops a cargo pod every 30 seconds with what the pad lacks, so packs arrive with no click. A force may have only one cargo landing pad per planet; if you already have one, add a request for space science packs to it and build only the inserter, chest and substation next to it. Supply electricity at the substation.
  • One cargo landing pad per planet: in a save that already has one, the blueprint pad cannot be built. Add a request for space science packs (1,000) to the existing pad and build only the inserter, chest and substation.
  • The chest stops at 2000 packs (10 stacks), the pad keeps up to 1000 and the platform hub up to its own cap; everything resumes by itself when robots take packs from the chest.
