Rocket-fuel supplier · 6/min

Makes 6 rocket fuel per minute from light oil: one chemical plant makes solid fuel and two assembling machine 2 make rocket fuel, on a red belt leaving the bottom row of the east side. One copy covers rocket supplies.

Native production test passed · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

← Space launch path · Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material at or above its panel figure, and connect electricity at P.
  4. Run the east exit belt straight to its consumer. When the consumer stops taking, the exit holds 8 and the machines stop by themselves.

Protocol: 15 min warm-up, 30 min measured, 10 min blocked (collection stopped, entrances still fed), 5 min settle, 10 min recovery. Every entrance was fed at its panel figure, not saturated. Blocked: rocket fuel exit held exactly 8, and no machine crafted in the last minute.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; one chemical plant (solid fuel from light oil) and two assembling machine 2 (rocket fuel), no modules, red belts, fast inserters, one substation. WEST: light oil 660/min into the top-left pipe; it feeds all three machines. EAST: one red belt exit on the bottom row, 6 rocket fuel/min, one machine per lane. Solid fuel is handed machine to machine. The two solid-fuel arms are wired to the exit belt tile and run only while it holds fewer than 8 rocket fuel, so the machines stop soon after the consumer stops taking fuel and restart by themselves; keep that red wire and condition. Supply electricity at the substation.
  • Place this supplier west of rocket supplies with its exit row (row 7 from its top) on the rocket-fuel entrance row and join them with a straight red belt. One copy covers rocket supplies (it takes about 5 rocket fuel a minute).
  • Feed light oil at or above the panel figure; extra simply stays in the pipe.
