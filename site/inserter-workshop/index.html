On-demand inserter workshop · four arms

Four on-demand tiers: normal, long-handed, fast and bulk inserters. Supply iron plates, copper plates and plastic; circuits and gears are made inside. Space Age stack inserters require additional ingredients and are outside this four-tier workshop.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

22 × 15 tiles · 134 entities · 12 AM2 · 0 chemical plants · 3 substations · red transport · no installed modules

Import & build

  1. Download or copy the complete import, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build all saved entities and preserve filters, stock controls, chest caps and circuit wires.
  3. Supply every numbered input and fluid port; connect external electricity at P. Let internal buffers prime.
  4. Remove finished goods for continuous output. Full storage intentionally pauses manufacture.

Targets describe continuous collection, not production into full storage. Recovery rates measure restarted collection after blocked outputs. Native measurement: 10 minutes; blocked 5 minutes.

Each product has its own saved provider cap. Keep the normal and fast inserter loop reserves available for downstream upgrades. After storage blocks, collect the finished products, restore all supplies and power, and allow the loop to refill before measuring.

  • Explicit compact on-demand alternative: bulk 2.5/min, not the requested continuous 5/min. One 22x15 ring with 12 assemblers; it replaces the first 26x17 version with 13.
  • Supply: with all four chests emptied at once the workshop makes more than its targets and draws about 630 iron plates, 275 copper plates and 7 plastic a minute; give it that. At exactly the recipe figures for the targets (422.5, 192.5 and 5) the normal and fast tiers stay above target and the long-handed and bulk tiers fall below.
  • Direct cable into both green assemblers, rear cable across the ring into the second green assembler, and a long-arm cable feed into red circuits. A buried branch of the iron entrance feeds both gear assemblers directly and rises inside the ring, where one arm lays iron on the ring's outer lane.
  • Independent one-stack provider caps: 50 each; normal reserve 2 and fast reserve 2 on loop before export.
  • No intermediate injection; baseline bulk hand 2, fast bonus zero.

If a product fails to restart: check the numbered supplies, power, recipe unlocks, saved wire connections, output cap and remaining chest space. Do not replace stock controls with unlimited feed.
