3 Take the ground parts from the kit

Sixteen passive provider chests on the kit's east side, one item each, filled to the cap and then stopped. The counts come from the qualified platform and receiver strings (SHA-256 f4cc0325a68e… and 00456afec81e…). 10% rounded up on fast-inserter and space-platform-foundation, zero on everything else.

Take out only what you build on the ground: the rocket silo and the receiver's parts (1 cargo landing pad, 1 fast inserter, 1 passive provider chest, 1 substation). Leave the starter pack and the platform parts in the kit's chests; the silo's robots collect them from there (step 4). Whatever you take out, the kit makes again.

First launch. In a save that has never created a space platform, the asteroid collector and crusher recipes are still locked: creating the first platform unlocks them. The kit then fills 14 of its 16 chests and waits (in an exploration run of this string the starter pack was ready at 28.8 minutes and the rocket silo, last, at 65.5 minutes). Do not wait for a complete kit: go on to steps 4 and 5 as soon as the silo and the starter pack are in their chests. Once the platform is created the kit makes the crusher and the four collectors by itself (in a second run with the recipes unlocked at minute 75, the crusher was done 14 seconds later and the fourth collector 17.9 minutes later), and the last cargo rocket waits for them. The 68.75 minutes in step 2 is for a save where a platform already exists.

4 Place the rocket silo

Place the silo from the kit and tick “Automatic requests from space platforms” on it once; it is off by default. With it on, the silo takes its cargo from the local logistic network, so the rocket silo and the kit's chests must be inside one roboport network with logistic robots. The kit makes no roboports, logistic robots or requester chests: build them first, for example from the robot essentials or the robot logistics workshop. Cargo cannot be put into the silo by hand while the setting is on.

Feed the silo from the three rocket-supplies chests: with robots (a requester chest and an inserter for each, with the rocket-supplies chests in the same network) or by hand. One rocket takes 50 rocket parts: 50 × processing unit, 50 × low density structure, 50 × rocket fuel. The chests hold 400 of each, so they can stock the campaign before the silo is built.

5 Create the space platform

On the silo, click “Build new space platform”. In “New space platform” choose Nauvis as the planet and the space platform starter pack, then Create. The silo's robots bring the starter pack from the kit's chest and the silo launches it first, in its own rocket. Creating the first platform unlocks the asteroid collector and crusher recipes: on a first launch the kit makes them now, and the last cargo rocket waits for them.

8 Packs arrive

Measured over 120 minutes: 60 space science packs per minute into the receiver's chest (60 made in orbit). First pod 64.03 s after the platform was built. With the chest not emptied it stops at 2000, the pad at 1000 and the hub at 2060 (cap 2000, ceiling 2100); no pack is crafted while it holds, and everything resumes by itself when packs are taken.
