Essentials mall · eight building products on demand

Eight building products on demand: assembling machine 2, fast transport belts, fast underground belts, fast splitters, fast inserters, bulk inserters, electric furnaces and electric mining drills. Each product stops at its own chest cap. Gears, cable, advanced circuits and the yellow-tier parts are made inside.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

23 × 17 = 391 tiles · 192 entities · 19 assembling machine 2 · 2 substations · red transport · no modules

Needs supplied electronic circuits: 84 per minute at the targets (measured draw 194.35 per minute). 1 copy of the green-circuit supplier (360 per minute each) covers it. Green-circuit supplier →

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep filters, circuit wires, stock caps and chest settings as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material and connect electricity at P.
  4. Collect the finished goods on the east. A full chest or exit pauses that product on purpose.

Protocol: 10 min warm-up, 20 min measured, 5 min blocked, 5 min settle, 10 min recovery. Recovery is the rate after collection stops and restarts. This mall is on-demand: the floors are minimum rates while all eight chests are emptied, not a fixed output.

Setup notes

  • Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age. 19 assembling machine 2 (no modules), red belts, fast/long-handed/bulk arms. WEST inputs, one labelled entrance each: iron plates, electronic circuits, copper plates, steel plates, stone bricks, plastic bars. EAST: eight passive provider chests in one column, one product each; each stops at its own cap, set in the constant combinator beside the fast-belt chest (edit one number). Gears, cable, red circuits, yellow belts, undergrounds, splitters, inserters and AM1 are made inside. Preserve the red ring wires, the green cap wires, all arm filters, the entrance belt conditions, the gear clock, the fast-inserter reserve decider and the fast-inserter feed decider. Supply electricity at the marked substation.
  • Feed each west entrance with its labelled material only; the harness feeds every entrance as a full red belt. Iron mostly goes to the rear belt behind the bottom row, which feeds three iron-gear-wheel machines and the underground-belt, assembling-machine-1 and transport-belt machines. The electric-mining-drill, splitter, inserter and fast-inserter machines take iron from the ring, which gets it through one admission arm fed by a bulk arm that lifts iron off the rear belt.
  • Electronic circuits are a supplied input (84/min at the floors, more when one product is drawn alone); one green-circuits supplier covers this mall.
  • Caps live in one constant combinator next to the fast-belt chest (one negative number per product). Each chest arm runs only while its product is below its cap.
  • Fast inserters are also a bulk-inserter ingredient. The fast-inserter machine's inserter-feed arm runs while the fast-inserter chest is below its cap or the ring holds fewer than 4 fast inserters (one decider), so the bulk-inserter machine keeps getting fast inserters while that chest is full. A second decider lets the fast-inserter chest arm take only fast inserters above a ring reserve of 14.
  • Gear arms of the six gear-hungry finals take turns in an eight-window clock (7.5 s each); yellow belts, inserters and AM1 take gears freely because their own ring limits bound them.
  • Plan supply above the entrance panels if all eight products should hold their floors at once: the panels are recipe totals at the floors, and products early on the ring run above them and take material first. Exploration measurements (full-length runs with a throttled-feed copy of the harness, not part of the qualification): with every entrance fed at exactly its panel figure and all eight products drawn, assembling machine 2 made 0/min (floor 2) and bulk inserters 0.25/min (floor 1); the other six held their floors. The floors need about the measured draw: 195 electronic circuits, 722 iron, 38 steel, 27 stone brick, 30 plastic and 30 copper per minute, two to three times the panels. Circuits alone limited to 360/min (one green-circuits copy) with every other entrance unlimited kept all eight above their floors. With less supply the mall still fills every chest in turn; only the all-eight-at-once floors are lost.

Reproduce

From factorio-command-center/ in the source repository:

export FACTORIO_RAW=<Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age data-raw-dump.json>
node scripts/essentials-mall/generate.mjs        # writes blueprint-sources/essentials-mall/essentials-mall.txt and manifest.json
node --test tests/essentials-mall.test.mjs
node scripts/essentials-mall/validate.mjs        # full native protocol; needs a local Factorio 2.1.24 install, run headless with a private profile under .cache/essentials-mall/
