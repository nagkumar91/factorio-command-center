Advanced-circuit supplier · 187.5/min

Makes 187.5 advanced circuits per minute (measured 187.5) from copper plates, iron plates and plastic bars, and sends them out on the east side on one red belt. It makes its own intermediate parts inside.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

66 × 14 = 924 tiles · 412 entities · 41 assembling machine 2 · 5 substations · red transport · no modules

Circuit suppliers: Green circuits · Advanced circuits · Processing units

Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep the inserter filters, the underground pairs and the dead-end belts as saved.
  3. Feed each numbered entrance on the west with its labelled material (see Inputs) and connect electricity at the substation marked P, at tiles (5–6, 5–6).
  4. Take the advanced circuits from the exit on the east, (65, 0). A full exit pauses the supplier on purpose.

Setup notes

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; 25 assembling machine 2 on advanced circuits, 5 on electronic circuits and 11 on copper cable; no modules, no beacons; red belts, fast, long-handed and bulk inserters, 5 substations. WEST: copper plates 600/min (row 1, top plate belt), iron plates 375/min (row 2, top plate belt), plastic bars 375/min (row 5, aisle) and copper plates 337.5/min (row 13, bottom plate belt); each may arrive on both lanes. EAST: one red belt exit on row 0, 187.5 advanced circuits/min. Keep the plate filters, the bulk arms between each cable machine and its circuit machine, the stub at (1,7), the underground pairs and the dead ends at the east ends of the belts. Supply electricity at any substation.
  • Belt the exit east to the advanced-circuit entrances of the consumers. The exit holds 7 circuits when nothing takes them, and every machine then stops.
  • Feed each entrance at least its panel figure; extra plates and plastic back up on the entrance belts.
