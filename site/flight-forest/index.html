Forests & pollution

For your modded Factorio 2.1.24 flight save. Normal quality · AM2 · fast inserters · red belts · no modules or logistic robots.

Download all four blueprints as one book

Renewable trees, not unlimited pollution removal. The autonomous module renews seeds, replaces lost trees and includes solar power after its initial bootstrap. The separate nursery and manual grove remain optional alternatives. Infrastructure still costs supplied materials. A standing forest needs enough space and surviving trees to match your factory’s emissions. No fixed blueprint can absorb every possible factory’s pollution.

Recommended: autonomous forest maintenance

Use the autonomous maintenance blueprint below for unattended operation. It keeps the tower powered and harvests only when replacement seeds are needed. Idle towers do not emit continuously in the installed game; the native idle probe confirmed this. It requires no continuing wood export, seed deliveries, fuel or grid power.

  1. Research Agriculture, Tree seed availability, Automation 2, Circuit network, fast inserters, red belts, solar panels and accumulators. Build the complete module, including the seed-reserve circuit wire, 6 solar panels and 6 accumulators. No clock, power switch or combinators are required.
  2. Place it on unobstructed natural planting terrain on Nauvis. Put 50 tree seeds into the marked seed chest once. Leave all other inventories empty. Start in daylight or wait for dawn. The initial canopy and full reserves take several growth cycles; allow about one hour to establish.
  3. Leave the seed and wood chests alone. The tower harvests only when the seed chest contains fewer than 80 seeds. Harvested wood becomes seeds; reserve seeds return to the same tower. When reserves recover, harvesting stops and the trees remain standing. No material disposal or ongoing manual collection is needed.
  4. The seed chest is wired directly to the tower. Its harvesting condition is Tree seed < 80; planting remains enabled. Lost trees are replanted automatically from the reserve. If the reserve falls below 80, harvesting resumes until enough wood has been processed back into seeds. Otherwise the canopy stands and the machinery waits.
  5. Keep the red seed-reserve wire intact. Do not replace the tower’s condition with unrestricted harvesting. Leave solar panels and accumulators connected. External clean grid power is optional, but the tested module needs none. Avoid dirty electricity if you want the measured pollution balance to apply.
  6. Scale by copying the entire module with non-overlapping planting footprints. This maintains a bounded forest; it does not expand itself, rebuild destroyed machinery, defeat enemies, or recover from losing all trees and all seeds. Keep it protected.

Infrastructure and optional manual alternatives

  1. Research: unlock Agriculture by mining iron stromatolite after its prerequisites. Confirm the Tree seed recipe is available; your early unlock may already provide it. Otherwise Tree seeding research is required. Also unlock Automation 2, fast inserters, red belts, Circuit network, solar panels and accumulators. Blueprint placement does not unlock research.
  2. Make the infrastructure: supply the workshop’s seven labeled chests with iron plates, copper plates, steel, electronic circuits, spoilage, stone and batteries. Connect electricity. It makes agricultural towers, AM2s, fast inserters, medium/big poles, steel chests, solar panels, accumulators, belts and display panels. Gear wheels, wire, sticks, AM1s, basic inserters and landfill are made internally. Collect final products from the individual upper chests. This workshop is tested for filling stock chests, not guaranteed simultaneous continuous throughput; leaving early-product chests full lets downstream machines receive supplies. Keep circuit wires and limits intact; chest bars only limit storage.
  3. Place one nursery: use an open area with natural terrain. Supply 50 tree seeds to the marked seed chest (craft from 100 wood). No ongoing wood, seed, fluid or fuel input is intended. The solar array must be unobstructed; startup at night waits for daylight. Leave its 16 panels and 14 accumulators connected.
  4. Wait through growth: trees take 10 minutes each to mature. The tower sends wood to the reserve chest; the AM2 turns 2 wood into 1 seed, and the return belt feeds the tower. Each harvested tree yields 4 wood, enough for two seeds. This is a renewable material balance once harvest starts, not instantaneous production.
  5. Take only surplus: keep at least 20 seeds in the seed chest and 100 wood in the wood reserve once established. The nursery pauses when its buffers fill; taking surplus wood or seeds lets it resume. No fuel burner destroys surplus. Pollution damage or attack can still require replacement seeds.
  6. Plant the pollution forest: place planting-only groves around and between polluting factories and the cloud edge. Supply about 50 seeds and grid power to each grove. Its saved circuit setting disables harvesting. Once planting finishes, you may remove the agricultural tower, inserter, chest, panel and pole to reuse the equipment, leaving the trees intact. Removal is optional: an idle tower was measured to emit no further pollution. This manual alternative lacks the autonomous module’s seed-renewal loop. Do not clear the trees with a deconstruction planner.
  7. Expand and maintain: repeat groves without overlapping planted areas. Replacement of trees after removing towers is manual. Keeping these simple planting towers permanently allows unattended replanting only while seeds are supplied. Planting and harvesting cause energy use and pollution; idle towers did not emit in the native probe. Choose the autonomous module above when you also want automatic seed renewal. Neither design expands itself or has unlimited resistance to pollution damage.

How much forest?

The installed planted-tree prototype absorbs 0.001 pollution/second = 0.06/minute per tree. The tower prototype’s 4/minute emission setting is activity-scaled, not a continuous idle cost. In the native idle test, its total pollution stopped increasing after planting ended. The separate native probe measured about 100 kW during planting and 0 W while idle; cumulative tower pollution stayed at 9.79444468 from minute 5 through minute 30. The autonomous module harvests only on demand, making its steady canopy a net absorber under the tested loss rate. Planting, harvesting, seed assembly and dirty electricity still add emissions. Solar power removes fuel emissions, not those operation costs. See the separate idle-mechanics probe.

Autonomous sizing uses ceil(factory emissions × margin ÷ measured net removal per module). The measured net rate already subtracts that module’s tower and assembler emissions; do not add those again. Passive sizing uses ceil(emissions × margin ÷ 0.06); include any separate nursery emissions in that case. Both are planning estimates, not guarantees: trees must be in polluted chunks, survive damage, and remain planted. Read the in-game pollution production statistics and map overlay, then expand where pollution escapes. Do not count trees outside the cloud as current absorption.

Natural soil also helps. Grass-1 absorbs 0.00108/min per tile; sand-1 absorbs 0.0009/min per tile in this profile. Concrete and stone paths lack that soil absorption. Keep the forest unpaved. Efficiency modules in miners and machines, clean electricity, fewer idle emitters and efficient layouts reduce how much forest you need; those modules are optional outside these blueprints.

Placement and troubleshooting

  • Terrain: planted trees allow grass-1 through grass-4, dry-dirt, dirt-1 through dirt-7, red-desert-0 through red-desert-3, and sand-1 through sand-3. These are the installed plant’s tile restrictions. Water and paving are not suitable; do not assume landfill becomes valid planting terrain without checking the tile in game.
  • No planting: confirm seed recipe/research, seed supply, electricity and suitable free terrain. Tree collisions, cliffs, water, rails and buildings reduce capacity. The displayed green footprint is approximate.
  • No harvesting: the standing-grove blueprint deliberately disables it. The autonomous module pauses harvesting when its seed reserve is sufficient. The nursery harvests mature trees, but a full wood output or seed buffer will pause the cycle.
  • No seeds: check the AM2 recipe, its electricity and wood reserve. Never remove the entire seed supply or empty all reserve wood during startup.
  • Workshop stalled: supply every input, especially spoilage for towers and 50 stone per landfill. Input chests are one unique place per material. Only basic workshop fixtures must be built upfront; it cannot create its starting assemblers or raw materials from nothing.
  • Factory cloud still growing: add standing trees near polluted chunks and reduce emissions. A tower’s planting footprint is small; copying nursery blueprints indefinitely is not the same as building a net-absorbing forest.
