Robot logistics workshop · 10/10/1/4/4 per minute

Manufacture construction robots, logistic robots, roboports, yellow storage chests and passive providers. Supply iron, copper, steel, plastic, sulfuric acid and lubricant; all intermediates are made inside.

Native production tested · Passing production and recovery evidence matches this exact import and prototype.

106 × 24 tiles · 918 entities · 53 AM2 · 3 chemical plants · 14 substations · red transport · no installed modules

Import & build

  1. Download or copy the complete import, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build all saved entities and preserve filters, stock controls, chest caps and circuit wires.
  3. Supply every numbered input and fluid port; connect external electricity at P. Let internal buffers prime.
  4. Remove finished goods for continuous output. Full storage intentionally pauses manufacture.

One shared passive-provider chest receives all five products. Preserve independent per-product caps so one product cannot consume all storage and every product stays available. Leave shared chest slots unrestricted. Keep the saved output controls connected and remove finished goods for continuous output.

  • Official 2.1.24 Space Age; AM2, no modules, red belts, fast, bulk and long-handed inserters. Four single WEST solid entrances, two WEST fluid entrances; one shared EAST passive provider with independent caps. All circuits, engines, batteries, frames and steel chests made internally. Preserve red loop stock wires, green chest wires and five filter-decider circuits. Supply electricity at P.
  • Leave shared chest slots unrestricted. Caps: 20 construction, 20 logistic, 2 roboports, 10 storage, 10 passive provider. Feed at least 20% above listed steady requirements while filling buffers.

If a product fails to restart: check the numbered supplies, power, recipe unlocks, saved wire connections, output cap and remaining chest space. Do not replace stock controls with unlimited feed.

Deploy the manufactured robots

Insert the manufactured construction and logistic robots into a powered roboport. Items sitting in the output chest are not deployed robots. Join powered roboports into a network and place chests within the relevant coverage.

The yellow storage chest receives network items; the passive provider offers goods to the network. Building this workshop needs no requester chests. Supply the workshop through its declared belt and fluid entrances.
