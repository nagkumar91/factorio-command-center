Oil products supplier

Makes petroleum gas 7,140, sulfuric acid 500, lubricant 150 and sulfur 15 per minute from crude oil, water and a few iron plates, plus light oil on request (up to 660 per minute, at the cost of petroleum gas). Petroleum gas, sulfuric acid, lubricant and light oil leave by pipe, sulfur on a red belt.

Native production tested · every required scenario passed for this exact import string.

43 × 15 = 645 tiles · 280 entities · 7 oil refineries · 11 chemical plants · 2 storage tanks · 3 substations · no modules

Suppliers: Green circuits · Advanced circuits · Processing units · Plastic bars · Oil products

Open in the library

Import & build

  1. Copy the import string, then use Factorio’s Import string.
  2. Build every saved entity; keep the mirrored refineries, every pipe-to-ground pair, both pumps with their conditions and the red wires as saved.
  3. Bring crude oil, water (pipes) and iron plates (belt) to the three west entrances (see Inputs) and connect electricity at the substation marked P at (5, 1)–(6, 2).
  4. Take petroleum gas, sulfuric acid and sulfur from the east edge, and lubricant and light oil from the north edge (see Outputs).

What happens in each case

  • Design rates: petroleum gas 7,140, sulfuric acid 500, lubricant 150, sulfur 15; all 7 refineries at 84 crafts per minute. Petroleum gas beyond a medium plastic supplier's 6,000 is the surplus: 1,140.
  • Sulfuric acid not drawn, then drawn again: the refineries run at 79 crafts per minute (84 at the design rates); the others carry on: petroleum gas 7,140, lubricant 150, sulfur 15. After the block they return to the panel figures.
  • Lubricant not drawn, then drawn again: the refineries run at 84 crafts per minute (84 at the design rates); the others carry on: petroleum gas 7,140, sulfuric acid 491.3, sulfur 15. After the block they return to the panel figures.
  • Sulfur belt not taken, then taken again: the refineries run at 84 crafts per minute (84 at the design rates); the others carry on: petroleum gas 7,140, sulfuric acid 500, lubricant 150. After the block they return to the panel figures.
  • Petroleum gas not drawn, then drawn again: the refineries run at 13.4 crafts per minute (84 at the design rates); the others carry on: sulfuric acid 500, lubricant 150, sulfur 15. After the block they return to the panel figures.
  • Light oil drawn at 660: light oil 660, so petroleum gas falls from 7,140 to 6,703.3 per minute (436.7 less; every unit of light oil drawn is light oil not cracked, two thirds of it in gas, computed 440); acid, lubricant and sulfur are unchanged and water in falls to 12,225.6.
  • Feed at 80% (crude oil 6,720, water 10,304, iron 8): petroleum gas 5,653.7, sulfuric acid 400, lubricant 150, sulfur 15; every machine kept working.
