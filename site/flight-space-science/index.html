Make white science in Nauvis orbit

A stationary Space Age platform can collect asteroids, make space science, and deliver it to a Nauvis cargo landing pad. You can place and manage the platform from remote view; you do not need to ride a rocket.

Factorio 2.1 / Space AgeAM2 · no modulesDesign target: 60 packs/minOffline instructions
Validation status: the compact orbital assembler cell below was tested in Factorio 2.1.24 on a real zero-gravity Nauvis platform. It delivered 150 packs to the platform hub during a 10-minute run (15/min). The integrated asteroid collection, crushing, and Nauvis delivery system remains a design plan and has not been throughput-tested end to end.

Tested blueprint · Orbital white-science cell

Import the blueprint string and place it on connected platform foundation near the hub. The west-facing inserter feeds space science directly into the hub; no chest is used.

Import and measured result

  • Download the tested blueprint string (28 entities; 14 × 15 tiles; one AM2; no modules).
  • Feed iron plates, carbon, and ice on the three south-side belts, left to right. Measured demand is 6 iron plates/min, 3 carbon/min, and 3 ice/min.
  • Measured output: 15 space science packs/min inserted into the platform hub.
  • Open the native Factorio test report. It records the exact blueprint SHA-256 and the 10-minute zero-gravity run.

Scaling

  • Place four cells, each with its own three input belts and hub-facing output inserter, for a 60 packs/min assembler capacity.
  • At that rate, supply 24 iron plates/min, 12 carbon/min, and 12 ice/min total.
  • The cell test validates assembly, electrical connection, and transfer into the platform hub. It does not validate asteroid collection, crusher balance, rocket requests, or delivery to a Nauvis landing pad.

Build order

Research Rocket Silo

That enables the platform-start trigger in this save. If the platform technology still says “Available,” the starter pack has not yet been launched from a rocket silo.

Launch a Space Platform Starter Pack

In the rocket silo, choose the new-platform action and select Nauvis orbit. This creates the platform hub and starter foundation, and completes the platform trigger.

Place the platform factory blueprint in remote view

Place foundation and machine ghosts. Enable requests for missing construction materials from Nauvis. The platform hub automatically requests the items needed to build those ghosts.

Craft an Asteroid Collector

This triggers the Space Science Pack technology in the flight save. Assign the collectors to Nauvis-orbit asteroids and filter one collector line for each chunk type.

Request packs on Nauvis

At the cargo landing pad, request space science packs. Add an unloading bay if you want inserters to move packs directly to a belt or train.

Blueprint A · Nauvis launch and receiving station

Place beside your rocket-silo logistics area. The landing pad is the output buffer; it does not need an extra chest.

Launch side

  • 1 rocket silo with access for the platform starter pack and construction supplies.
  • Use hub construction requests to supply the orbital ghosts from Nauvis. If the silo uses logistic requests, it can pull those requested items automatically once your logistic network is available.
  • For the first launch, select “Build new space platform” and choose Nauvis. Later launches can deliver requested buildings and materials.

Return side

  • 1 cargo landing pad on Nauvis, requested item: space science pack.
  • Request 1,000 packs for a useful local buffer (about 16 minutes at 60/min).
  • Place an unloading bay beside the pad, then a fast inserter to your science belt or lab feed.
  • Keep the pad inventory able to accept deliveries; a full pad can spill cargo onto the ground.
Factorio 2.1 rockets can carry multiple item types in one launch. The platform can drop requested items to Nauvis in cargo pods; returning space science does not consume a return rocket.

Blueprint B · Compact stationary orbital science factory

Build a connected foundation patch, keep the platform parked over Nauvis, and route the three ingredients into a four-machine assembler bank.

Asteroid and smelting block

  • 3 asteroid collectors on the platform edge: one filtered for metallic chunks, one carbonic, one oxide.
  • 3 crushers, one dedicated to each chunk type. Use the matching basic crushing recipe.
  • After each crusher, use a filtered splitter to return matching asteroid chunks to that crusher’s input loop.
  • Route metallic crushing’s iron ore through 1 electric furnace to an iron-plate belt.
  • Route carbonic crushing’s carbon and oxide crushing’s ice to their own belts.

Science and power block

  • 4 AM2 assemblers, all set to Space Science Pack, no modules.
  • Bring iron plates, carbon, and ice to the assembler input side; merge the finished packs onto one output belt to the hub.
  • Put a cargo bay beside the hub for additional platform inventory.
  • Start with about 40 solar panels for headroom. Space has no day/night cycle; check the platform power graph after the crushers and assemblers are running and add panels if needed.
  • No poles are required on a platform. Its whole foundation is one electric network.
Chunk-return loops matter: crushers can return asteroid chunks as a by-product. Split those chunks back to the matching crusher so they do not block the useful output or accumulate in the hub. If the splitter priorities or loop starve a crusher, the pack rate will fall.

Balanced production at 60 packs/min

2 iron plates+1 carbon+1 ice→5 space science packs / 15 s

The recipe runs at 0.75 crafting speed in an AM2: 3 crafts/minute × 5 packs = 15 packs/minute per assembler.

Starter pack and one-time supplies

60 platform foundations+20 steel plates+20 processing units→1 Space Platform Starter Pack

The starter pack creates the hub, 36 foundation tiles around it, and 10 foundations in hub inventory. It does not include the collectors, crushers, furnace, assemblers, belts, or solar panels in this plan. Those are requested from Nauvis as the orbital ghosts are placed.

Blueprint construction inventory

3 asteroid collectors, 3 crushers, 1 electric furnace, 4 AM2 assemblers, 40 solar panels to start, fast inserters, red belts, 3 filtered splitters and enough platform foundations to connect the factory footprint.

Platform guardrails

Keep all foundation connected to the hub. Collectors must sit on an edge. Chests, roboports, and robots do not work on platforms; use the hub and cargo bays for storage. Stationary Nauvis orbit is the safe, simple location for this factory.

What you should see when it works

  1. Metallic, carbonic, and oxide chunks are collected separately.
  2. Crusher output loops return chunks to the matching crusher.
  3. Iron plates, carbon, and ice reach all four AM2s without backing up.
  4. The shared output belt inserts space science into the platform hub.
  5. The Nauvis landing pad’s request is satisfied, and delivered packs unload toward your labs.
A useful first check: temporarily watch the factory for several minutes. If one ingredient belt empties while the others back up, the platform’s delivered rate is set by that ingredient. Increase collection or processing on that branch before adding more assemblers.
