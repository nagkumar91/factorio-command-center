# Native probes for the two space-launch suppliers

Small headless runs that prove the game mechanics the concrete and rocket-fuel suppliers are built on (Factorio 2.1.24 Space Age, private config under `.cache/concrete-supplier/probes/`). Run from `factorio-command-center/`: `node scripts/concrete-supplier/probes/run-probe.mjs lanes 43205` or `... fluidsides 605`. Results land in `results/`.

- `lanes.lua` → `results/lanes.json`: which lane a fast arm fills (`[left, right]` item counts on the end tile of an east-running belt after the belt backs up). An arm dropping onto a belt that runs straight away from it fills the right lane (`away` `[0,4]`); an arm north of an east belt fills the south (right) lane (`north` `[0,4]`); an arm west of a south-running column or of the corner tile where it turns east fills the north (left) lane (`column`, `curve`, `concrete4` `[4,0]`); an arm north of an east belt plus a stub side-loading it from the north fill both (`sideload` `[4,4]`). It also shows how far a crafter fills its output before it stops (`full_output`) when it has **no output arm** (bare machine fed by script): concrete 100, rocket fuel 20, solid fuel 50. These are not the limits in the built layouts, where an output arm takes from the machine: in the native qualification runs the concrete machine stopped with 30 to 45 concrete in its output (30 in the published 7 x 5 layout, 35 in the earlier 9 x 4), the rocket-fuel machines stopped with 4 each when the exit gate was removed (review run, SHA `553f26a8...`), and the chemical plant, which hands solid fuel on by arm, stopped at 50.
- `fluidsides.lua` → `results/fluidsides.json`: an assembling machine 2 takes concrete's water and rocket fuel's light oil on the side it faces, in all four directions; a chemical plant takes `solid-fuel-from-light-oil`'s light oil from either input corner, in all four directions.
