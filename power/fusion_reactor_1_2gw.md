# Fusion Reactor (1.2GW)

The supplied blueprint is preserved in [the import string](fusion_reactor_1_2gw.txt)
and [decoded JSON](fusion_reactor_1_2gw.json). Find **Fusion Reactor (1.2GW)**
under **Blueprints → Local** in the Command Center.

**Create construction crate** prepares one passive provider chest with the exact
152 construction items below. Copy its command and paste it into Factorio.
The shortcut keeps your existing crate-builder loadout. **Pack build** instead
adds these materials to that loadout for customization.

The same command is available in [this Lua file](fusion_reactor_1_2gw.crate.lua).
It creates a chest near your player and reports blocked placement or insertion
failures. Console `/c` commands disable achievements in that save.

## Starting and supplying it

**Plan operating supplies** opens an editable order of **20 fusion power cells**
and **4,000 units of cold fluoroketone**, matching the tested starting stock.
These are stock amounts, not a continuous consumption rate or a minimum charge.

1. Connect the substations to external electricity for startup. The reactors,
   cooling plants, and fuel inserters need power before the generator circuit
   can sustain itself.
2. Charge the reactor coolant circuits with cold fluoroketone. The native test
   inserted 1,000 units at each of the four reactors. Pipe the fluid into the
   connected circuits in normal play. If transporting barrels, add an
   unbarrelling assembler; the supplied layout does not include one.
3. Put 5 fuel cells in each requester chest to match the tested starting stock.
   Keep replenishing them. For automated delivery, connect all four chests to
   powered orange logistics coverage and supply logistic robots plus fuel in
   provider/storage chests. There are **no roboports in this blueprint**;
   your existing robot extension can provide the coverage.
4. Keep the return pipes intact. Reactors turn cold coolant into plasma;
   generators return hot fluoroketone; the four cryogenic plants cool it back
   down. Fresh hot coolant is not a continuous external feed, and cold coolant
   is not an exported product of this generator layout.

## Manufacturing the operating supplies

The website shows these recipes and their machines, links to matching
production blueprints, and expands the full chain with shared demand combined.
The following are base recipe amounts from the installed game data, before
machine or research productivity bonuses.

- **Fusion power cell**, in a cryogenic plant: 5 lithium plates + 1 holmium plate
  + 100 ammonia → 1 cell, 10 seconds at crafting speed 1.
- **Fresh fluoroketone**, in a cryogenic plant: 50 fluorine + 50 ammonia +
  1 solid fuel + 1 lithium → 50 hot fluoroketone, 10 seconds at crafting speed 1.
- **Cool it**, in a cryogenic plant: 10 hot fluoroketone → 10 cold fluoroketone,
  5 seconds at crafting speed 1. The four placed plants perform this cooling
  recipe; fresh coolant production and fuel manufacturing happen upstream.
- **Lithium:** holmium plate + lithium brine + ammonia → lithium; smelt lithium
  into lithium plates for fuel cells.
- **Ammonia:** separate ammoniacal solution into ammonia and ice. Handle the
  ice byproduct so separation can continue.

The planner traces these through holmium processing, lithium processing,
ammonia separation, and oil processing for solid fuel. Raw resources include
holmium ore, lithium brine, fluorine, ammoniacal solution, crude oil, stone,
and water. Resource availability, research, surface conditions, and enabled
mods still apply. The site's snapshot is from the installed Factorio 2.0.77
configuration, including Everything on Nauvis.

**Pack finished items** packs the solid fuel cells. Fluids remain in the plan
for pipe, tank, fluid-wagon, or barrel transport; loose coolant is never placed
in an item chest.

## Validation

[Native test report](fusion_reactor_1_2gw.validation.json), generated by
`npm run test:fusion` in a disposable Space Age save:

- Imported and placed all 152 entities from the exact supplied string; checked
  saved rotations and recipes.
- Executed the actual website crate Lua and counted all eight item types:
  exactly 152 normal-quality items in one passive provider chest.
- Added 20 fuel cells through the requester chests and a single 4,000-unit
  cold coolant charge. No further fuel or coolant was inserted during the test.
- Removed external startup power after 20 simulated seconds.
- Sustained an external **1.1 GW test load for a 10-minute simulation**, with
  all 24 generators working and all four cooling plants crafting. Every sample
  after the warmup supplied the full test load.

The original **1.2GW** name is retained. This test establishes the stated
1.1 GW load case; the plant's own electrical consumption reduces power
available to the rest of your factory. Automated fuel delivery and cold-world
heating were not part of this test; fuel was placed into the saved requesters
and the test surface did not freeze.
