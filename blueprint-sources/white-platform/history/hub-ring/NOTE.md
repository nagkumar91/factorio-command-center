# hub-ring (superseded, qualified)

The planner's prototype layout: 10 solar panels at the four corners and outward on every side, and 1 accumulator west of the north-west block. 283 foundation tiles, 22 x 22, requested weight 5,000 kg (five cargo rockets).

It passed the full protocol on 2026-11-21: 60.01 per minute collected and 60.00 made, 0 low-power ticks, average 928 kW, peak 1,640 kW, lowest free hub slots 17. The rocket check gave six launches of exactly 1,000 kg each, and the build took 16.0 min.

`bands-seven-panels` (280 tiles, 19 x 18) replaced it. That layout trades three panels for six accumulators at the same weight and puts the power in an east band and a south band. This report came from an earlier version of the harness (cargo pods in flight were still counted as platform entities), so its hashes do not match the current `control.lua`.
