## Internal supply and instance map

The two circulation lanes carry mixed ingredients. Filtered fast inserters take recipe ingredients; intermediate output inserters return only that station's product. Stock limits and one whole-loop inventory reader control admission. Finished output inserters transfer directly to the boundary providers. Keep the belt cycle and its green wire network intact.
