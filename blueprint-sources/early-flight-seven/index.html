Feed each labeled west inlet with its indicated raw ore or coal and connect external power. Red and green science stay together for nearby lab feeds. All use yellow belts, undergrounds and splitters, with no modules.

These are the separated layouts, preserving the original production cells. Separation lets you build each where it fits; it does not reduce their individual machine counts. Each exact string passed a native Factorio 2.1.24 production test (report): four hours of warm-up, 60 minutes measured with every inlet on a full yellow belt, then three hours with the output chest full (every inlet must stop) and 60 minutes of recovery. In the science workshop the two science assemblers set the rate; in the other six one iron furnace does.

The strings published on 16 November 2026 imported without working pole wires, so nothing in them was powered. These versions carry their own wires and keep every machine, inserter and chest of each cell; rows and columns that held only straight pass-through belts are cut out, and the science workshop's green cell now sits against the red one (31 × 52 instead of 37 × 57).
