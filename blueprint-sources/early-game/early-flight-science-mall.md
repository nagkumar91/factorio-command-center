# Early Flight science and red-belt mall

Blueprint import string: [`early-flight-science-mall.txt`](early-flight-science-mall.txt)

One narrow stamp composes eight raw-input cells from the Early Game library:

- Automation and logistic science packs, with their output chests aligned beside each other for lab feeds.
- Fast (red) transport belt, fast underground belt and fast splitter.
- Assembling machine 2, medium electric pole and big electric pole.

The cells use assembling machine 1, steel furnaces, yellow belts, yellow underground belts and yellow splitters. No modules are installed. Steel furnaces use the coal feed. Raw operating inputs are iron ore, copper ore and coal; power is external. Each labeled inlet is on the west edge. Several cells have their own labeled inlet for the same raw resource, so feed every matching inlet. A small set of yellow belts extends the green-science feeds to the west edge.

Connect external power once at the top `P` marker. The internal big-pole trunk connects all production cells. Output chests are on the east side of each cell and limited to one stack; empty them or belt the products out to resume crafting. Display panels can be skipped until Circuit network is researched.

Build the complete stamp after researching Automation, Logistics, Automation 2, Logistics 2, Advanced material processing, and Electric energy distribution 1. Earlier in the run, place only the ghosts for entities already unlocked.

## Size and composition

The entity-center bounds are 37 × 275 tiles. This is a slim, stacked layout built by joining existing library cells; it does not share their smelters or intermediate-production machines. The two science outputs sit on the same row, 21 tiles apart. The other finished-item chests are grouped along the east edge.

It contains 32 assembling machine 1s, 17 steel furnaces, 1309 yellow belts, 152 underground-belt endpoints, 30 yellow splitters and 8 one-stack wooden output chests. The power trunk adds 23 big electric poles. The blueprint has 22 labeled raw-material inlets across the independent cells.

The source cells were library designs for Factorio 2.0.77 / Space Age with matching per-cell evidence. Their assemblers were converted from AM2 to AM1 for this request, and the cells were composed with a shared power trunk and aligned science outlets. This composite has not had a native combined production test; rates are intentionally not claimed. Lower assembler speed reduces the source cells' original output rates.
