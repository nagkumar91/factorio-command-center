# Orbital space science cell

One assembling machine 2 making space science packs on a space platform in Nauvis orbit, fed by separately built
asteroid processing. The white-platform design supersedes it for a whole platform.

- Blueprint: `orbital-space-science-cell.txt` (SHA-256 `3336b5bf6f89f42a54e04e200e31eabf010c1c1ca852c94a402ab38e28122486`), generated by `node scripts/flight-space-science/generate.mjs`.
- 10 entities: 1 assembling-machine-2, 4 fast-inserter, 3 fast-transport-belt, 2 solar-panel. No modules.
- Footprint 7 x 6 tiles; 34 foundation tiles under entities.
- Inputs on the south edge, left to right: iron-plate at (5.5, 3.5) 6/min, carbon at (6.5, 3.5) 3/min, ice at (7.5, 3.5) 3/min.
- Output: the west-facing inserter at (4.5, 0.5) puts packs straight into the platform hub on the west side.
- Power: two solar panels, 360 kW in Nauvis orbit. Add panels anywhere on the platform at locations with less space solar.

## Native qualification

Passed for this exact string in Factorio 2.1.24 (`validation/native-report.json`), each run on its own zero-gravity Nauvis platform, 10 measured minutes:

- Stated feed (6 iron plates, 3 carbon, 3 ice per minute): 15 packs/min into the hub (150 in 10 minutes), crafts exactly 1200 ticks apart, about 156 kW average.
- Abundant feed (both lanes of every entrance belt kept full): 15 packs/min into the hub (150 in 10 minutes), crafts exactly 1200 ticks apart, about 159 kW average.
- Hub full for 5 minutes, then cleared: 16 packs/min into the hub (160 in 10 minutes), crafts exactly 1200 ticks apart, about 115 kW average. Packs moved during the block: 0.

Earlier versions are kept under `history/`.
