# buried-runs (78 x 60 = 4,680 tiles, qualified, superseded)

The 78 x 60 layout after the review fixes: a second bury pass tunnels the manifold's straight runs that
nothing touches (29 pairs, 147 belts), string SHA-256 `24bd5d0d…1ff666`. It passed the full native
protocol with the same rates as the unburied string. Its `manifest.json` and `native-report.json` are here.

Superseded by the 78 x 56 layout, which moves the rail cell (rail, stick, rail) from below the production
row into the south-west manifold corner. The rail belt there gets iron side-loaded on its north lane at
the west end, the two rail machines drop rails on its south lane, and it runs east along rows 51-52 into
a curve at the west end of the production row's rear belt. The processors' circuit belt now starts at
column 0, fed down column 0 from a circuit tap at row 44, and its east end turns north. Rows 53 to 56
(the old rail cell, its stone and steel belt and the 114-tile steel detour) are gone.
